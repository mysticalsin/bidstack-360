# Dashboard win rate with one closed quarter

**Problem:** On the demo book, Aritzia (won) and NOS (lost) both close in Q2 2026. Performance → Win rate replaced that 50% with "Needs closed bids in two quarters." An open bid such as Rush was never part of the rate.

**Fix:** One closed quarter shows the rate and how many closed bids it covers. Two quarters keep the trend line. No closed bids says so.

**Why:** A trend line needs two points. The win rate of the closed book does not.
