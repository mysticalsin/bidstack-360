# Visitor demo hosts sample company info

`seedOrgData` is the workspace a public demo sign-in creates. It wrote the CRM company and an enrichment row, and no firmographics, job postings, or posts. The company strip reads the stored intel summary, so revenue, open roles, and news stayed "Not refreshed yet" until a live source was connected.

`hostedIntelSummaryInput` builds a sample revenue estimate from the account's own approximation, three fixture postings seen yesterday, and one sample post. Live sources stay off. No filing period is written. `seedVisitorDemoCompanyIntel` stores those rows and rebuilds the summary. Fixture postings already stored for another account, including the Pfizer whitespace fixture, are summarised too. Each sample posting gets its own fetch time, because MSA whitespace orders postings by that time and hashes the order. Northwind's sample titles stay off IT, engineering, and digital. That account's HEMA MSA is the whitespace fixture, and IT-shaped roles collapse its open lines to one. Dismissing that last line leaves it on the flag. The stored rows are not a live fetch.
