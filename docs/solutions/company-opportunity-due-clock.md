# Company opportunity due clock

The company Opportunities tab received each bid's due date and did not show it. On the demo book an open bid such as Logistec SOC 2 (due 2026-08-15, still in flight) looked current. A won or lost bid keeps that past date because the deal already closed, so the same chip would call it overdue.

The tab now shows the shared due clock on open bids, including due today. Won and lost bids say the clock stopped.
