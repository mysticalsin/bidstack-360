# Performance Breakdown opens the chart's own bids

The dashboard Performance card draws three different books. Revenue is won actuals plus the open forecast. Win rate is closed bids. Top accounts are won plus open. Breakdown used to open won-only on revenue and every bid on the other two tabs, so a lost bid appeared under top accounts and the open forecast disappeared from revenue.

`performanceBreakdown` names the book. The drill keeps that book until a status chip replaces it.
