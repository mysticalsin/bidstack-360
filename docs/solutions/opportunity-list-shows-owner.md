# The opportunities list names who owns the bid

Demo bid OP-2041, CI Financial — IT Modernization & MSP, is owned by alex.example@example.test. The list row showed the customer and the updated time. The owner email is already on the record and was not printed. A bid with no owner has nothing to show.

The row prints "Owned by" plus that email. The line uses the secondary foreground so it stays readable on the row. A blank owner leaves the line off. The list does not load a new field.
