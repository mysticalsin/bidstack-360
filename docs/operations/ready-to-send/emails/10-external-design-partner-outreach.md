# Email/LinkedIn DM 10 — Cold Outreach: External Design Partners (5 Variations)

These are LinkedIn DM templates for reaching out to potential design partners (pilot customers) outside the Mantu network. Use these for cold outreach to RevOps leaders, VP Sales, CRO, or Head of Presales at target accounts.

**Research checklist before sending:**
- [ ] Verify the person is responsible for CRM / sales tools
- [ ] Note their current CRM (mention it specifically in the message)
- [ ] Find one genuine signal of pain (e.g., a post they shared about sales process, AO/RFP frustration, CRM switch)
- [ ] Connect first (LinkedIn connection request) before DMing if not already connected

---

## Variation 1 — Generic B2B / RevOps pain

**Best for:** RevOps Leaders, VP of Sales, CRO

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I came across your profile while researching [their industry] companies managing complex sales cycles.
>
> We're running a private pilot for **BidStack 360°** — a CRM built specifically for bid and presales pipeline management (the part Salesforce / HubSpot handles poorly). 8 weeks, free access for your team, direct line to our product team.
>
> Not a demo request — I'm specifically looking for teams with ≥ 5 people in presales or bid management who'd be willing to give honest feedback in exchange for shaping the product.
>
> Is this something that could be a fit for [Company]?

---

## Variation 2 — RFP / AO-specific pain signal

**Best for:** Bid managers, tender teams, VP Presales at consulting firms

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed [Company] operates in [consulting / IT services / public procurement] — which usually means a meaningful volume of RFPs and tenders to track.
>
> We've built **BidStack 360°**, a CRM designed around that workflow: pipeline tracking, document version control for proposals, team collaboration on bid responses, win/loss tracking per opportunity.
>
> We're selecting 5 design partner organizations for a free 8-week pilot before our commercial launch. Direct influence on the roadmap + lifetime 30% discount if you decide to continue.
>
> Would [Company]'s bid or presales team be a candidate? Happy to send a 2-page overview.

## Variation 3 — CRM frustration signal

**Best for:** Someone who posted or commented about CRM frustration / switching / evaluation

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I saw your [post / comment] about [Salesforce/HubSpot] — it resonated.
>
> We built **BidStack 360°** specifically to fix what traditional CRMs get wrong for B2B sales with long cycles and proposal-heavy processes: pipeline stages that map to real bid milestones, built-in document handling, email and calendar sync that actually stays clean, and team-level opportunity collaboration.
>
> We're in private pilot mode — 5 organizations, 8 weeks free, your feedback shapes the product.
>
> Worth a 20-minute call to see if it's relevant for your team?

## Variation 4 — Warm (shared connection or event)

**Best for:** Someone you met at an event, or a 2nd-degree connection via a mutual

> Hi [First Name],
>
> [We met at / We're both connected to / I saw your talk at] [event/person] — I've been meaning to reach out.
>
> I'm working on **BidStack 360°**, a B2B SaaS CRM focused on bid and presales pipeline management. We're running a pilot program for 5 organizations before our commercial launch — 8 weeks free, direct product influence, 30% lifetime discount if you continue.
>
> Given [Company]'s focus on [their relevant domain], I thought it could be a fit.
>
> Would you be open to a quick 20-minute call to see if it makes sense?

## Variation 5 — LinkedIn InMail (longer, more formal)

**Best for:** Paid InMail to a VP/C-level you're not yet connected with

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Apologies for the cold outreach — I'll keep this brief and specific.
>
> I'm [Your Name] at Mantu Group. We're launching **BidStack 360°**, a B2B SaaS CRM designed specifically for organizations that manage complex sales cycles, bid responses, and proposal pipelines — the use case where Salesforce and HubSpot both fall short.
>
> We're running a private design partner program: 5 organizations get 8 weeks of free access, a direct monthly session with our product team, and a 30% lifetime discount if they subscribe after the pilot. In return, we ask for structured feedback (a 45-minute session at week 4 and week 8) and honest product input.
>
> Based on [Company]'s profile and your role overseeing [their function], I believe [Company] would be an ideal design partner. The pilot starts in approximately 3 weeks.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes for a call this week or next to see if this is a fit?
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> **[Your Name]**, BidStack 360° / Mantu Group
