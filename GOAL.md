## 1. The Ultimate Goal

The goal of BidStack 360° is to dominate the **Bid and Pre-Sales lifecycle** by providing a "360° Intelligence" layer that standard CRMs (Odoo, Salesforce, Twenty) lack.

We don't just track leads; we **augement the decision-making process** to ensure Mantu (and its clients) only bid on what they can win, and win everything they bid on.

### The Three Pillars of Excellence

1.  **Intelligence (The Brain)**: Leveraging Dust AI and MCP to provide real-time, predictive insights (Win probability, Competitor Radar, Financial Health).
2.  **Cinematics (The Body)**: Achieving an "Apple-standard" UX. This means Liquid Glass interfaces, 120fps animations, and a spatial grid that feels alive and premium.
3.  **Reliability (The Soul)**: An enterprise-grade, secure, and scalable architecture (Node 24, Fastify, Prisma, PostgreSQL 16).

## 2. How We Can Improve It (The Roadmap)

To reach the "Ultimate CRM" status, we must execute on the following improvement vectors:

### A. Functional Parity (Odoo & Twenty)

- **Invoicing & Financials**: Implement a lightweight but powerful invoicing module that pulls deal values (in micros) directly into professional, Apple-style PDF templates.
- **Modern Relational UX**: Adopt Twenty's "Settings-as-Code" and highly relational navigation—where every entity (Company, Contact, Opportunity, Invoice) is interconnected with zero friction.
- **Project Management**: A "Bid Project" view that tracks resource allocation (who is writing the technical proposal?) and deadlines.

### B. Deep Intelligence Evolution

- **Autonomous Research Agents**: Instead of a "Ask Dust" button, have Dust agents automatically "sweep" the web every 24 hours for every open opportunity and update the intelligence ribbon.
- **Collaborative Decision Matrix**: Turn the Bid/No-Bid Matrix into a collaborative tool where multiple stakeholders (Sales, Tech, Finance) vote on criteria, with AI resolving conflicts.
- **Predictive Pricing**: Analyze historical `closed_won` data to suggest the "Golden Price Point" that maximizes both margin and win probability.

### C. The "Apple Standard" UI/UX

- **Liquid Glass Design System**: Fully implement `backdrop-filter` with dynamic saturation. The UI should look like it's floating over a cinematic background.
- **Spatial Navigation**: Use `Command + K` for a global command center that allows users to jump anywhere, run AI commands, or change stages without clicking.
- **Micro-interactions**: Every button click, stage change, and data update should have a tactile, spring-physics animation (Framer Motion).
- **Haptic Feedbacks (Web)**: Use subtle visual "pulses" and sound design (optional) to signify successful state changes.

### D. Architectural Hardening

- **Idempotency & Resilience**: Implement `Idempotency-Key` headers for all mutations to prevent double-billing or double-updates in high-latency environments.
- **Real-time Presence**: Show who else is viewing an opportunity in real-time (Presence API) to prevent collision.
- **Zod-driven Performance**: Move all validation to the edge and ensure the API response times stay <100ms through aggressive DB indexing and caching.
